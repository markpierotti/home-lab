## Working folders

`Inbox/` holds captures written by Hermes. `raw/` holds preserved source bytes and capture manifests. `Review/daily/` holds machine-generated capture receipts; `Review/proposals/` holds interpretations awaiting a decision. `Wiki/` holds approved durable knowledge. `_archive/` holds superseded notes.

The Slim vault is authoritative. The separate Mac/iCloud vault is a one-way reading copy. Mac edits are not pushed to Slim; conflicting local files are preserved by the mirror under `.sync-conflicts/`. This is not a bidirectional sync system.

Captured history begins on 20 October 2026. Previously pruned data cannot be recovered by creating this vault.
